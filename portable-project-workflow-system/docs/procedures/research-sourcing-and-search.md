# Research, Sourcing, And Search

## Trigger

Use when researching factual claims, sources, terminology, competitors,
keywords, search intent, structured metadata, or generative-search behavior.

## Evidence Classes

Keep these separate:

- primary or authoritative factual sources;
- secondary explanatory sources;
- competitor or search-result discovery evidence;
- tool estimates and correlations;
- owner/business facts;
- implementation-derived repository evidence;
- qualified external conclusions.

A competitor or optimization tool may identify a topic; it does not become
factual authority merely by ranking or recommending a term.

## Workflow

1. Define the research question, jurisdiction, date sensitivity, and decision
   it will support.
2. Search bounded authoritative sources first when material facts are
   involved.
3. Record publisher, title, canonical URL, access date, supported claims,
   limits, and ownership in a source ledger.
4. Build an inventory and intent map before creating overlapping pages or
   records.
5. Treat volume, difficulty, term-frequency, AI-visibility, and competitor-gap
   outputs as estimates or correlations, not search-engine requirements.
6. Confirm that the content or artifact is stable enough for an optimization
   pass. When active owner review is likely to overwrite tool-driven edits,
   defer those edits unless the research is needed to make the current owner
   decision.
7. Present findings and tradeoffs before changing content when the research
   pass was authorized as research-only.
8. Apply approved findings case by case through the normal content or product
   workflow.

## Search And Content Boundaries

No tool establishes a mandatory keyword count. Natural language, user value,
source entailment, safety, intent ownership, and owner-approved positioning
override mechanical frequency recommendations. Do not introduce filler,
unsupported claims, or copied competitor structure to improve a score.

## Completion Evidence

Record queries or inputs, sources reviewed, tool/date/location settings,
limitations, findings, affected records, non-decisions, and the approval
needed before implementation.
