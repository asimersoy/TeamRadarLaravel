# Content Lifecycle

## Trigger

Use for public copy, labels, help text, metadata, editorial pages, structured
content records, or corrections to those materials.

## Authority And Roles

- The project content strategy and record specification own intent and scope.
- The assigned writer drafts or corrects within an explicit packet.
- The implementation agent independently owns QA, integration, and Git.
- Owner and specialist approvals remain separate gates where assigned.

Record the current writer before dispatch. No historical writer assignment is
valid by itself.

## Record Contract

Before drafting, define the record's stable identity, audience, search or user
intent, answer-first purpose, required structure, source ownership, relations,
status, review flags, and publication boundary. Use a matching content-record
template rather than prose that cannot be imported or reconciled.

A title, slug, route, canonical URL, or stable-identity change is a migration,
not a copyedit. Record redirects or aliases, internal references, related
records, imports, metadata, tests, and re-review impact before closing it.

## Workflow

1. Preflight intent, overlap, source sufficiency, sensitivity, and target
   rationale without drafting the record.
2. Review and authorize the preflight.
3. Draft from approved sources and decisions.
4. Validate structure and source entailment without adding filler merely to
   satisfy a count.
5. Independently review factual claims, natural language, user value, safety,
   metadata, relationships, and structured payload.
6. Issue bounded corrections when required.
7. Keep the record unpublished until all publication gates pass.
8. Commit a coherent reviewed batch locally before another batch begins,
   unless the owner explicitly requests a checkpoint.

The default starting batch for genuinely routine, comparable content is three
records. Sensitive, source-uncertain, medical, legal, safeguarding, privacy,
ranking, eligibility, money, crisis, or diagnosis-adjacent content starts at
one record. A visible experiment or decision may change these defaults; worker
convenience may not.

## Editing Rules

- Review replacements in context; do not use unsafe blanket substitutions.
- Directly answer the user's intent and avoid defensive or process-narrating
  filler.
- Prefer one useful purpose per record. Review overlap and intent ownership
  before adding similar pages or moving wording among them.
- Targets are strict during ordinary drafting. When owner-approved material is
  retrospectively removed because it adds no value, a target may be revised
  through a visible decision rather than padding the remaining content.
- A validator proves only its measured structure.
- Content corrections never rewrite Git history.
- After importing or rendering content, scan public output for placeholders,
  draft labels, internal review language, development state, and other
  implementation details that should not reach users.

## Editorial Quality Standard

- Make headings user-facing and useful. A heading should name the question,
  decision, or information the section actually provides.
- Give a title's primary answer or most useful information first. Do not build
  a summary from a secondary subsection merely because it is easy to extract.
- Use one sentence for a straightforward definition and a second only when it
  adds essential value. Practical summaries normally keep two or three central
  points rather than reproducing the full recommendation set.
- Keep routine qualifications, classification details, publisher names,
  research status, referral language, and defensive caveats in the body unless
  they are central to the title. Retain a compact safety statement only when
  immediate serious harm is materially connected to the topic.
- Do not manufacture curiosity with false truncation, hidden text, blur, or
  ellipses when nothing was cut. Use a clear continuation cue when the
  interface needs to show that fuller content exists.
- Write natural language for the target audience. Reject translation-shaped
  phrasing, abstract comparisons that do not help a person act, and sections
  that mostly explain limitations instead of providing value.
- Do not tell users to ask obvious questions merely to fill a page. Include
  guidance only when it reduces real uncertainty or prevents a material error.
- Verify public claims about routing, forms, availability, pricing, ranking,
  independence, neutrality, and service behavior against actual approved
  system and business behavior. Silence is better than an unsupported durable
  promise; required commercial influence must still be disclosed truthfully.
- Prefer definite wording when the verified behavior is definite. Use modal or
  conditional language only for genuine uncertainty or variability.

## Completion Evidence

For each batch record retained versus rewritten totals, validators, source and
intent checks, rendered checks where applicable, review flags, database/import
state, reconciliation results, and local commit.
